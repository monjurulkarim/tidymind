TidyMind Privacy Policy

Short version: everything runs on your device.

TidyMind runs all AI on your device. Files, file names, and file contents are never uploaded, never sent to any server, and never used for analytics — there are no analytics and no accounts. Nothing is collected, shared, or sold.

The only network use

  • The one-time AI model download from Hugging Face over HTTPS, when you choose to download a model. The download contains model weights only — no information about you or your files is sent.
  • Apple's own StoreKit service, which handles purchases and receipts if you choose to buy TidyMind Pro. That communication is between your device and Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

There is nothing else: no analytics, no crash reporting to third parties, no tracking, no advertising, no accounts, no cloud.

What stays on your device

Everything the app touches — your folders, file names, document contents, images, AI-generated plans, and your settings — is processed locally and stored only in the app's container on your Mac. Deleting the app removes it all.

Permissions

  • Folders you choose: TidyMind only reads and writes folders you explicitly pick. Access is granted per folder through the standard macOS open panel.
  • Network: used only for the model download described above.

Children

TidyMind collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
