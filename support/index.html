Frequently asked

Where do the AI models come from, and do they need the internet?

Models (roughly 1–5 GB) download once from Hugging Face into the app container. After that, everything runs locally on your Mac — organizing, reading documents, and renaming never touch the network.

I changed my mind after applying a plan. Can I undo it?

Yes. Every organize and rename run is undoable: open History → Undo next to the session and your files return exactly where they were, including nested folder structure.

What does Pro unlock, and how do I manage or cancel it?

Scanning, AI analysis, and full plan review are free forever. Pro unlocks the payoff actions: applying organize plans, applying renames, saving & re-applying Schemes, and removing duplicates. It's available as an annual subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase — manage or cancel anytime through your App Store account.

Which Macs are supported?

TidyMind requires macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon (M-series) — the on-device MLX engine runs on the GPU/Neural Engine stack those Macs provide.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple as well — visit reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple ID you used.

Is my data really staying on my device?

Yes — see the Privacy Policy. No analytics, no accounts, no servers. The only network use is the one-time model download and Apple's own StoreKit for purchases.
